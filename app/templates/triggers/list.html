8 Database Triggers Architecture

Production DBMS evaluation: 8 non-identical triggers across 8 canonical domain tables with zero audit table overhead.

Stored Procedures Hub
LIFECYCLE Triggers 1 & 2

Club Size Automation

Automatic increment (Trigger 1) and decrement (Trigger 2) of Clubs.member_count.

GOVERNANCE Triggers 3 & 4

Governance & Status

Enforces single-president rule via SQLSTATE 45000 and dynamic count sync on active/inactive toggle.

INTEGRITY Triggers 5 & 6

Event Integrity & Reviews

Rejects retrospective registration on past events and blocks unverified feedback submissions.

PROVISIONING Triggers 7 & 8

Lifecycle & Provisioning

Auto-progresses event status to Ongoing upon attendance and auto-provisions baseline ClubProfile.
